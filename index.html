About LUMA

A place where you belong.

LUMA Laser and Electrolysis is a studio created by Jill Sherman and Emma Schutte.
We offer laser hair removal, electrolysis, and skin treatments with a focus on thoughtful
care, expertise, and results.

Founded on the belief that clinical shouldn't feel cold, we've created something different.
Our space is a reflection of who we are—thoughtful, warm, and personal. It's designed to
feel less like a place of service and more like an extension of our home. A place where you
can settle in and feel comfortable.
