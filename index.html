About LUMA

A place where
you belong.

Luma Laser and Electrolysis is a studio created by Jill Sherman and Emma Schutte. We offer laser hair removal, electrolysis, and skin treatments with a focus on thoughtful care, expertise, and results.

Founded on the belief that clinical shouldn't feel cold, we've created something different. Our space is a reflection of who we are—thoughtful, warm, and personal. It's designed to feel less like a place of service and more like an extension of our home. A place where you can settle in and feel comfortable.

Skin Treatments

Gender Affirming Care

We proudly offer a welcoming, inclusive, and affirming space for LGBTQIA+ clients seeking electrolysis and laser hair removal services. We understand that gender-affirming hair removal is a deeply personal journey, and our goal is to offer compassionate care tailored to your needs.
