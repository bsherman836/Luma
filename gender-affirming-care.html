Our Approach

What is gender affirming care?

Gender affirming care is personal care designed to help people feel more comfortable, confident, and aligned with their identity and body. For many transgender and gender diverse individuals, hair removal can be an important part of that process — whether for confidence, daily comfort, self expression, or surgical preparation.

For many transgender women, hormonal changes can lead to thicker or darker hair growth in unwanted areas, making personalized care essential. For transgender men, testosterone therapy can cause new or increased hair growth in various areas of the body, which laser hair removal and electrolysis can help address.

At Luma Laser & Electrolysis, we tailor every treatment to your unique skin type, hair type, goals, and transition journey. Whether someone is seeking facial hair removal, beard reduction, body hair reduction, electrolysis for surgical preparation, or long-term permanent hair removal, every treatment is approached with care, discretion, and respect.

We’re committed to creating a safe, affirming environment where clients feel seen, comfortable, and supported throughout the process.

The Process

How it works.

01
Free Consultation

We start with a complimentary 15-minute phone consultation to understand your goals, areas of concern, and where you are in your journey. From there, we’ll recommend the right treatment approach and build a personalized plan designed around your body, comfort level, and timeline.

02
Personalized Plan

Together, we’ll build a treatment plan tailored to your hair, skin, transition goals, and timeline. Whether you’re preparing for surgery, addressing facial hair, or working toward long-term reduction, every plan is paced around what feels right for you.

03
Treatment Sessions

Sessions are calibrated to your goals. Whether laser hair removal, electrolysis, or a combination, treatments are delivered with precision, comfort, and discretion. Most sessions take 15 to 60 minutes.

04
After Your Session

The treated area(s) may feel a bit warmer and sometimes slightly red for a few hours up to a day or two. Hair shedding will happen after 1 to 3 weeks, so we recommend exfoliating weekly, shaving in between sessions, and keeping your skin moisturized.
