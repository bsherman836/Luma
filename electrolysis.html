The Treatment

What is electrolysis?

Electrolysis is the only FDA-approved method for truly permanent hair removal. A fine probe is inserted into each individual follicle, where a precise electrical current destroys its ability to produce new hair — without affecting the surrounding skin.

Because each follicle is treated individually, electrolysis works where laser can’t — on every hair color, including white, gray, blonde, and red, and across the full spectrum of skin tones. Every plan is built around your goals, your skin, and your hair.

The Process

How it works.

01
Free Consultation

We start with a complimentary 15-minute phone call to learn about your hair, your skin, and your goals. We’ll walk you through what to expect from electrolysis and build a personalized treatment plan around you.

02
Treatment Session

A fine probe is inserted into each individual follicle, delivering a precise electrical current that destroys its ability to grow new hair. Sessions range from 15 minutes to 3 hours depending on the area being treated.

03
Recovery & Follow‑Up

Mild redness or slight swelling around treated follicles is normal and typically resolves within a few hours. We’ll send you home with aftercare guidance and schedule your next session, usually one to two weeks later.

04
Lasting Results

Each treated follicle is permanently disabled — that’s why electrolysis is FDA-recognized as the only truly permanent hair removal method. Most clients see complete clearance after 8–15 sessions, with no ongoing maintenance needed afterward.

Pre-Treatment

Before your appointment.

A few simple steps before your electrolysis session help you stay comfortable and let us treat each follicle as effectively as possible. Please follow these recommendations once you’ve scheduled your appointment.

Complete forms & get
cleared for treatment.

No waxing, tweezing, or

24 hours before.

Post-Treatment

After your appointment.

Some redness, slight swelling, or tiny pinpoint marks around treated follicles is normal and typically settles within a few hours. These simple steps help your skin heal cleanly between sessions.

No strenuous exercise
for 24 hours.

No saunas or hot tubs
for 48 hours.

Apply a cool compress
to soothe any redness.

Use fragrance-free
products like aloe vera.

Avoid unprotected sun
for at least 2 weeks.

Don’t pick or scratch
any small scabs.
