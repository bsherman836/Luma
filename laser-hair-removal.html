The Treatment

What is laser
hair removal?

Laser hair removal is a medical-grade procedure that uses concentrated light to permanently reduce unwanted hair. The laser targets the melanin (pigment) in each hair follicle, converting light energy into heat that disables the follicle’s ability to produce new growth — all without affecting the surrounding skin.

At Luma, we use advanced laser technology that allows us to safely and effectively treat a wide range of skin tones and hair types. Every treatment plan is tailored to your unique skin, hair color, and treatment area — so you get results that are both lasting and safe.

The Process

How it works.

01
Free Consultation

We start with a complimentary 15-minute phone call to assess your skin tone, hair type, and treatment goals. We’ll walk you through the full process, answer your questions, and build a personalized plan.

02
Treatment Session

During each session, a calibrated laser is passed over the treatment area. The light energy is absorbed by the hair follicle and converted to heat, disabling future growth. Most sessions take 15–45 minutes.

03
Recovery & Follow-up

There’s no downtime. You may notice mild redness or sensitivity for a few hours after your session — this is normal and resolves quickly. We’ll give you personalized aftercare instructions and answer any questions.

04
Lasting Results

With each session, you’ll notice progressively less hair regrowth. Most clients achieve 80–95% permanent reduction after completing their full treatment plan. Occasional maintenance sessions may be needed over time.

Pre-Treatment

Before your appointment.

Before your laser hair removal appointment, these are the safety and pre-care measures
that will ensure you have a great experience. Please make sure to follow these recommendations
once you've scheduled your appointment.

Complete forms & get
cleared for treatment.

No waxing or tweezing for 4 weeks.

No sun exposure or sunless
tanner for 2–4 weeks.

No aggressive skin treatments
for 2–4 weeks.

Only use gentle skincare — no retinoids or chemical exfoliants 1–2 weeks before.

Shave 24–48 hours before
your appointment.

Arrive early and with clean,
product-free skin.

Post-Treatment

After your appointment.

Your skin might be a little red or tender after your laser hair removal treatment, and that's
perfectly okay. Here's what you'll need to do to nurture your skin post-treatment and help your
side effects dissipate quickly.

Avoid strenuous exercise
for 24–48 hours.

No saunas, hot tubs, or
steam rooms for 48 hours.

Keep the treated area clean
with a gentle cleanser.

Apply fragrance-free, soothing
products like aloe vera.

Avoid unprotected sun exposure
for at least 2 weeks.

No shaving or exfoliating
for 10 days post-treatment.
